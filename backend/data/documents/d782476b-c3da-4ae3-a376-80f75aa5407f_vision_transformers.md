# Attention Mechanism and Vision Transformers
Published: 2024-03-15
Authors: Vaswani and Zhang

Vision Transformers (ViT) apply self-attention mechanisms to image patches, achieving 91.5% top-1 accuracy on ImageNet-1K.

## Performance Comparison
Under low-light conditions, ViT accuracy drops to 84.2%, whereas specialized ResNet models maintain 87.0% accuracy.
